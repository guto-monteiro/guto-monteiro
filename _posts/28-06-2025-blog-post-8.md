This summer, I had the great opportunity to visit Sweden and join the Nexus of Organic Matter Analytical Developments (NOMAD) Summer school coordinated by [Jeffrey Hawke's](https://www.jeffreyhawkes.com) group.

We stayed at the Tjärno Marine Laboratory and had the opportunity to interact with many renowned researchers from around the world. Many thanks to all the teachers for the amazing opportunity!

We had the opportunity to learn a great deal about fluorescence-excitation matrix analysis, high-resolution mass spectrometry, NMR, and isotopic tracing.
